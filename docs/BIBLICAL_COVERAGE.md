# Biblical coverage map

The 66-book Protestant canon is interpreted through simultaneous thematic families. These are grouped adaptations, not 66 separate mechanics. Deuterocanonical books and literal coverage of every incident remain outside this version. All families can coexist; none is a mission or completion stage.

| Family | Source books | Theme | Functional consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| division | Genesis, Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers, Deuteronomy | Creation, separation, covenant, wandering | Waiting divides water and adds memory. |
| exile | Joshua, Judges, Ruth, 1 Samuel, 2 Samuel, 1 Kings, 2 Kings, 1 Chronicles, 2 Chronicles, Ezra, Nehemiah, Esther | Belonging, power, genealogy, exile, return | Returning after absence opens a passage and changes the letter addressee. |
| lament | Job, Psalms, Proverbs, Ecclesiastes, Song of Songs | Suffering, attention, song, uncertainty, love | Stillness opens the ceiling and shifts reality. |
| bones | Isaiah, Jeremiah, Lamentations, Ezekiel, Daniel, Hosea, Joel, Amos, Obadiah, Jonah, Micah, Nahum, Habakkuk, Zephaniah, Haggai, Zechariah, Malachi | Judgment, displaced presence, restoration, resistance | Attention in the crypt removes the throne and grows roots. |
| table | Matthew, Mark, Luke, John | Embodiment, hospitality, vulnerability, resurrection | Remaining together before the angel restores a crossing and roots. |
| letters | Acts, Romans, 1 Corinthians, 2 Corinthians, Galatians, Ephesians, Philippians, Colossians, 1 Thessalonians, 2 Thessalonians, 1 Timothy, 2 Timothy, Titus, Philemon, Hebrews, James, 1 Peter, 2 Peter, 1 John, 2 John, 3 John, Jude | Witness, community, reconciliation, endurance, hospitality | Repeated separation and reunion stabilizes geometry and redirects a letter. |
| renewal | Revelation | Open gates, river, living tree, renewal | Roots and accumulated memory open gates and the water crossing. |
