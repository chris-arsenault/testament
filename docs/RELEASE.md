## Limits

The Bible is interpreted through seven grouped thematic families of the 66-book Protestant canon, not one mechanic for every book or event. Deuterocanonical books are not included. Impossible connections use authored threshold transitions rather than live recursive portal rendering. Geometry is procedural and stylized.

Combination checks establish valid states and exits, not exhaustive balance or artistic success. Physical touch hardware, subjective headphone listening, and an independent human playthrough remain unverified. Those limits do not prevent playing the published build.
